Join DAR's Team 1640, Sab-BOT-age, to take on a full-size robot build. Mechanical design, wiring, programming, and strategy all have to work together when the robot hits the field.

High school students design, program, and build industrial-sized robots to play an action-packed game, released in January. They compete on a themed field as part of a three-team alliance in the spirit of Coopertition®. In 360-degree learning guided by adult mentors, each FIRST® Robotics Competition team also creates a team identity, raises funds to meet its goals, and advances appreciation for STEM in its community.
