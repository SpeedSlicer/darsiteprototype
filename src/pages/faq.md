## Do I need experience?

No prior robotics experience is needed. Students learn with guidance from coaches and mentors.

## Which program should I choose?

Start with our [three programs](/programs): [FLL](/fll), [FTC](/ftc), and [FRC Team 1640](/frc). Contact DAR to confirm which team fits your student and has space available.

## How do I join?

[Visit the joining page](/join) and fill out the interest form. DAR keeps a waitlist and contacts families when openings are available.

## When can I visit?

Check the [calendar](/calendar) and [contact DAR](/contact-us) to confirm a visit. Meeting times vary by team and season.

## What does it cost?

[Ask DAR](mailto:info@darobotics.org?subject=Program%20costs) for current program fees 

## Can I help without being an engineer?

Yes. [Volunteer opportunities](/volunteer-opportunities) include technical mentoring, organization, logistics, and outreach.

## How can I sponsor DAR?

Read [sponsorship information](/sponsor) or visit [Support DAR](/support) for sponsorship and volunteering options.

## Who should I contact?

Email [info@darobotics.org](mailto:info@darobotics.org), or visit [Contact us](/contact-us).
