## Support student robotics

Downingtown Area Robotics brings students together through FLL, FTC, and FRC programs. Sponsorship helps provide the resources students use to design, build, program, and compete.

## Ways to sponsor

- **Financial support:** help with program materials, robot parts, and competition costs.
- **Professional support:** share expertise or services that help the programs operate.

Contact DAR before arranging a contribution so we can confirm current needs and discuss the options with you.
