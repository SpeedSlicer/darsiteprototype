## Become a mentor

Help students learn by doing. Mentors can support programming, mechanical design, wiring, CAD, and the process of testing and improving a robot.

## Every skill helps

You do not need an engineering background. Teams also need help with project management, purchasing, travel, food coordination, communications, and community outreach.

## Start a conversation

[Email DAR about volunteering](mailto:info@darobotics.org?subject=Volunteering%20with%20DAR). Tell us what you enjoy doing and when you are available. We’ll help you find a way to contribute.

[Explore the programs](/programs) or [contact us](/contact-us).
